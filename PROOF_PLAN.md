# PROOF_PLAN

The proof is an experiment that can come out against us. Hypotheses H1 to H7 are in HYPOTHESES.md and hash-locked before the first main model call.
Real model calls (E1 to E5) are cached by SHA-256 of the request in results/raw/. Simulations (S1 to S4, C*) are labelled simulated everywhere.
Every number in the paper comes from results/FACTS.json, built by scripts/facts.py from results/analysis.json.
Failure conditions that change the headline: see "What would count against us" in HYPOTHESES.md.
