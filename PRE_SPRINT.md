# PRE_SPRINT

Nothing in this repository existed before 15 November 2026. Code, data files, prompts, hypotheses, simulations, the
paper and the site are all sprint work, started during the AI x Epistemics Research Sprint.

Third-party prior work used, all cited:
- Lin et al. (2023), PNAS Nexus, domain quality ratings (public dataset, reuse permitted by the authors).
- The published description of Community Notes bridging-based ranking (open documentation). Our implementation is our own.
- Published descriptions of PageRank and TrustRank, MinHash, TF-IDF cosine similarity, Beta reputation and proper scoring rules.

AI assistants helped write code and drafts under Kinnoski's direction. See the LLM Usage Statement in the paper.
